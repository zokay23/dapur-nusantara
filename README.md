**Dapur Nusantara** — an Indonesian kitchen-rush game: customers stream into your warung ordering nasi goreng, mie ayam, sate ayam and gado-gado. Chop, fry, grill and boil each ingredient on the counter, assemble the dish on the plate, and ring the bell to serve it before the customer's patience runs out. Chain serves into combos, survive the dinner rush, and make it through the day with your three hearts intact.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in a desktop browser. Click ingredients on the counter (or press 1–8), click a finished station to plate it, and press Space or click the bell to serve. No network, no build step — the game is plain static files under `game/`.
